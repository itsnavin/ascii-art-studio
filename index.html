A QUICK FIELD GUIDE
A few tiny tips.
01 / DENSITY
Characters have weight.
A dot feels light. An @ feels heavy. Image palettes go from sparse to dense; dark pixels get the heavier characters.
02 / DETAIL
More is more. Sometimes.
More columns add detail and make wider art. Start around 80-100. High-contrast images with a clear subject work best.
03 / KEEP THE SHAPE
Give your letters space.
Paste into a monospaced font and keep every space. Image proportions account for tall character cells. PNG keeps the studio's exact spacing.
